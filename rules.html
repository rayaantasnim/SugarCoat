Severe Safety Red Lines

SugarCoat is built exclusively to sugarcoat embarrassing, benign human mess-ups. Sarcasm permanently terminates where genuine harm, illegality, or human suffering begins.

Prohibited Infraction Categories & Automated System Shutdown

Our underlying ethical models and client-side filter heuristics monitor text streams for irreversible real-world harms. If any submitted prompt contains, promotes, or flirts with any of the following non-negotiable red-line categories, the transformation pipeline immediately aborts:

☠️

Physical Violence & Murder

Any confessions involving physical assault, manslaughter, premeditated murder, terrorism, or domestic abuse are strictly banned. No exceptions.

🛑

Sexual Assault & Exploitation

Non-consensual behavior, harassment, grooming, stalking, and sexual crimes are completely rejected by all computational nodes.

💔

Self-Harm & Suicide

We never sugarcoat personal self-harm or suicidal ideation. Prompts touching self-harm trigger compassionate emergency crisis assistance links.

⚖️

Grand Theft & Extortion

Stealing cars, financial fraud, blackmail, armed robbery, or identity embezzlement are strictly excluded from comedic hype.

Automated Enforcement Mechanics:

Upon detecting any red-line infraction, the system will:
1. Immediately drop all active visual neon themes and revert to a muted neutral security state.
2. Freeze the runtime message counter to prevent further token processing.
3. Deliver a strict, non-negotiable Action Blocked: Severe Safety Violation banner.
4. Provide immediate crisis resources where self-harm is detected.
